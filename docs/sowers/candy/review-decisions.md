# Candy direction review decisions

2026-11-25. These are design judgments and geometric checks, not consumer-test results.

## First comparison: not accepted for promotion

The three generated concept directions communicate the desired cutesy candy idea, but the first actual procedural meshes do not yet have the same quality. [Actual v1 silhouettes](rejected-v1-shapes.png) and [actual v1 physical relief](rejected-v1-physical.png) are retained alongside the positive-master v1 source/export archive.

Scout's acute folded crown reads as damage and has a .061mm minimum sampled quadratic radius at 30mm scale. Bud's tall paired crown suggests rabbit ears and also misses the assumed .6mm contour target. Seed is the cleanest initial contour. All three have a largely blank lower body; dark face color supplies much of their identity. The one-color physical relief is materially weaker than the colored digital view. None is selected as a final marketable mascot.

## Second comparison: intended correction

Scout needs a larger, broader helmet over a compact tapered torso, rather than a narrow head on a T-shaped body. Bud needs low, outward leaves rather than tall paired peaks. Seed remains the simple organic control. Shared shallow helmet, face, eye and chest paths must carry identity without separate colored inserts. The actual positive geometry must be displayed directly so a shader cannot conceal a weak relief.

The color mascot may use ivory, citrus and a dark covered-face mask, but its physical study should remain legible in a single material. Digital animation can flex a connected form modestly; physical manufacturing evidence refers to the neutral master. No rig pose proves mold release or candy durability.

Acceptance for the next decision requires visual inspection of full-size and small silhouettes, side/front/back actual master views, regenerated source-pinned STL checks, and sustained interaction tests without resource growth. The supplier still needs to validate actual deposition, release, coating readability, shrink and material/process choices.

## V2 candidate: accepted for further testing, not final market acceptance

The larger Scout helmet now spans more than its short arm tabs; the torso and boot contour are compact. The actual rig is integrated into the watcher and the roster's vector marks. Ten mascot tests plus four independent physical tests pass, and the application suite totals 124 passing tests. The actual physical view no longer substitutes painted eye/visor inserts.

A same-material lighting/size study (`tools/sowers-relief-study.html`) shows that the .22mm eye rise is faint under frontal ambient light. Side/raking light strengthens it, so one attractive angle is not enough evidence of a robust candy face. Uniformly scaling the full master to 25mm and 20mm also scales down the already shallow features; those rows are visual alternatives, not separate approved manufacturing files. The next useful physical comparison holds the Scout XY paths fixed while testing more pronounced relief and upper perimeter blending.

The stable 15-minute interaction run remains a distinct acceptance check. It pins local sources, geometry and parameters and watches releases, errors and reloads. Its pass cannot establish consumer appeal, actual mold release, coating readability, or food-contact suitability.

## V3 lead: broad moderate relief

At 22:17 Pacific the coordinator promoted the exact broad-moderate study eye paths and surface depths to the shared canonical data, retaining the unchanged Scout outline and eye centers. The mask floor is now 5.45 mm and the eyes rise 0.40 mm to 5.85 mm within the same 30 × 6 mm envelope. The measured study crest arms are approximately 0.96 mm and crown stroke 0.98 mm; these are section measurements, not a universal minimum for every curved tip.

The moderate profile is the lead design judgment because its wider rounded arches read more clearly without the stronger profile's heavier relief. The stronger and top-edge-rounded masters remain separate controlled alternatives. Pinned light/size captures retain the exact candidate STL hashes and camera/light settings; the frontal view remains deliberately demanding. A four-cavity negative coupon is a proof-tool conversation aid, not validated production tooling.

The creator now separates animated character and actual candy master controls. The character has six distinct status poses at small sizes; motion is presentation only. Final immutable production stability evidence is still pending through 23:30 Pacific. No consumer preference, sour-sanding readability, release performance or manufacturing approval is claimed.
