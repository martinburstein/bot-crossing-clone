## Concept directions v1

Project asset: `public/sowers/candy/concept-directions-v1.png`.

Prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup / original mascot concept exploration. Create a high-end industrial character-design comparison board for SOWERS, an original cute sour-gummy candy brand whose mascot also represents helpful agents constructing a Mars outpost. The user rejected our former puffy astronaut as TOO BLOBBY. Explore THREE CLEARLY DIFFERENT strong marketable directions on one landscape board, evenly spaced in three columns on warm white: 1 SCOUT: small compact rounded-trapezoid astronaut with a bold rounded-rectangle helmet, clean bevels, a narrow dark mask framing two simple playful eyes, small integrated arms and a paired boot notch. 2 SEED: an upright seed-shaped masked explorer, top cut into one simple seed-tip/leaf suggestion, one smooth connected contour with tiny flipper arms and clean boot notch, restrained visor. 3 BUD: a compact sprout-shaped explorer with a blunt leaf crown integrated into the helmet outline, a broad shallow masked face, tapered body, stout connected feet. Each design should look precise and brandable, NOT swollen, chubby, balloon-limbed, Michelin man, bubble astronaut, grub, baby, marshmallow, or a collection of spheres. Favor broad flat facets with rounded corners, molded relief, crisp silhouette and friendly proportions. Keep the original Mars explorer aura without copying LEGO figures, logos, Wobbly Life meshes or existing candy mascots. Under each full-size matte 3D mascot, show the SAME EXACT silhouette as a realistic 30mm translucent sour gummy dusted with a little fine sugar, SINGLE COLOR citrus orange or lime green, one connected flat-backed relief piece with no detached ears, fingers, antennae or fragile parts. The candy and mascot must have matching outlines; mask and eyes are shallow relief on the candy, not black inserts, no floating bits. Show a tiny black silhouette icon for each at the bottom. Simple editorial labels only: SOWERS at top; SCOUT, SEED, BUD under columns. Tasteful modern rounded-bold typography, cheerful tangerine, acid lime, berry accent, premium but cutesy; no packaging claims, no nutrition, no price, no fake production-ready claims, no mold diagrams, no clutter, no comparison to old rejected character. The board should make the real physical candy translation immediately believable and the three directions easy to compare.
