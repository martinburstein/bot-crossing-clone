# Candy-first Sowers design criteria

The first avatar was rejected as too blobby. This run is an actual direction change, not a softer rendering of the same rig. Sowers should work as an agent mascot and as one recognizable molded sour candy.

Review each candidate at full size, 32px, in black silhouette, as a single-color relief, and on a small pack front. A distinctive contour and covered-face explorer cue matter more than model detail. Candy studies must show the same contour as the digital mascot. A sugar-dusted render cannot be used as evidence that fine features survive production.

A promising form has a small number of intentional surfaces, gently rounded corners, connected short arms/feet, and a simple mask/eye expression. Avoid inflated shoulder balls, layered cuffs, floating side pods, antennae, separate fingers and narrow decorative bridges. Seed/sprout symbolism should be part of the contour or shallow relief rather than an applied fragile stalk.

The Scout explores squared helmet/compact torso; Seed explores a tapered seed contour; Bud explores a blunt integrated leaf crown. They are design alternatives, not three independently validated products. Visual preference, candy-tool geometry, character motion and distant readability are separate acceptance checks. No consumer testing, trademark clearance, formulation development or proof-cavity trial is implied.

Manufacturing route and numerical clearances remain supplier-specific. A 30mm positive master is a dimensional conversation aid; it is not a food-contact mold or a production approval.
