# Sowers prototype research

User steering, 2026-11-26: all MAGI work now produces character prototypes. Build separate Mars Mission, Wobbly Life and Bot Crossing corpora before generating blends. The fourth supplied image is the preferred cream/orange/cocoa palette and cute helper personality anchor.

The soil-diagram analogy is a ternary mixture map. M / W / B recipe percentages always sum to 100. They specify art direction; they are not measured authorship or an empirical style classifier. Goat 4 remains a separate preference anchor rather than an invented measured mixture.

Private original attachments and SHA-256 manifest are preserved under `.cache/sowers-reference-corpus/`. Goat 2 has an AVIF payload despite its PNG filename; a lossless PNG decoding was made only for inspection. Reference imagery is research input, not a runtime texture or a copied character asset.

Corpora must distinguish direct visual observations, official corroboration, interpretation and unknowns. Every proposed Sower remains original: no copied LEGO connectors, printed planet insignia, Wobbly meshes or Bot Crossing mesh. Generated candy mockups are concept studies, not manufacturing evidence.

Creative work stops at the reported 2% allowance; remaining reserve down to 1% is for backup and handoff only.
