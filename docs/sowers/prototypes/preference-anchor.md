# Goat 4: preference anchor

Direct observation of the supplied `goat4.png`, preserved privately with its SHA-256. This is a Sowers concept board and the user's current favorite. It supplies a preference, not independent market evidence and not a fourth percentage axis.

## What carries the direction

- A broad rounded-square helmet has a clear protective rim, gentle crown and shallow face opening. Its width gives the figure a recognizable top-heavy outline without a mass of round limb blobs.
- The face is a single horizontal cocoa window with two large cream happy arches. The viewer can read friendliness without a mouth, eyebrows or tiny screen icons.
- Cream occupies the large shell/body regions. Citrus orange forms a crown stripe, two broad side panels and the lower boot/sole region. The color arrangement is orderly rather than decorative patchwork.
- A small orange seed/leaf mark sits in the central cream chest bib. It establishes Sower identity with one mark; the original Mars planet artwork is absent.
- Arms are short attached mittens. Two short feet are separated by one smooth shallow arch. The contour is compact and uncluttered.
- A smaller companion repeats the design. The size comparison makes it feel collectible and approachable. This is a composition observation, not evidence of actual physical sizes.
- The lower orange candy studies try to retain the helmet rim, facial arches, seed chest and boot split as relief. Sugar highlights help some edges and obscure others. These are generated concept illustrations, not physical proof of toolability or coating readability.

## Across the ternary exploration

Hold the warm cream/citrus/cocoa family and helpful emotional tone while deliberately varying head shape, mask coverage, visor/brow construction, body/limb proportions and pose. A Mars-heavy result can feel calm and alert without adopting the reference's anger. A Wobbly-heavy result can have reaching limbs without becoming an unstructured blob. A Bot-heavy result can be round and friendly while retaining the original seed mark and crown stripe.

Every character gets a paired single-color candy concept. If its identity depends on orange/cream separation, a thin antenna, disconnected hands or tiny equipment prints, record that weakness and simplify the actual shape rather than treating the render as a successful manufactured sample.
