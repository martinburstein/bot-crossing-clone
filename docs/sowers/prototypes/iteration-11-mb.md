# Iteration 11 — Integrated seed canopy

## Recipe

**M45 / W10 / B45** is an authored art-direction recipe, not a measurement or style classifier. Goat 4 remains a separate preference anchor.

- **Mars:** protective brow and contained face, simplified into the cowl edge.
- **Wobbly:** a very slight counterlean.
- **Bot Crossing:** friendly eyes and helper presence.
- **Fixed preference:** warm cream/orange/cocoa palette, broad orange crown stripe, pale arch eyes and original seed chestmark.

## Output review

The cream shell tapers over the crown, then widens across the face and shoulders into a clear canopy cowl. Its breadth and integration make it read differently from a conventional round helmet stacked over a tiny torso, especially around the shoulder silhouette. The orange brow stripe, compact cocoa face window, pale friendly arches and seed chestmark preserve Sowers identity. Short mittens attach at the lower sides, and the orange candy keeps the same broad connected canopy outline with shallow face, seed and boot relief.

The figure is nearly frontal rather than clearly three-quarter-front. An orange lower waist and boots remain distinct beneath the cowl, so integration is strongest across the head and upper body rather than down the entire figure. The candy has slight highlights and surface grain. These are concept-render limitations; the sheet does not establish physical size, candy legibility, tooling, tolerances or demolding.
