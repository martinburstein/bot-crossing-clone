## Blend guidance

- **High Wobbly influence:** rounded masses, sparse facial marks, broad color regions and pose readability.
- **Medium:** smooth head/torso/limb transitions and warm shading, interpreted through the separate goat4 palette anchor.
- **Low:** reduce extended limb proportions to one restrained pose cue; keep the shared Goat4 palette and original Sower shapes.

## Candy simplification notes

Long slim arms can become narrow bridges in a simplified candy form. Deep gaps beneath outward arms and between spread legs create undercuts and fragile-looking connections. Shorten or thicken limbs, bring arms closer to the torso, and round or shallow the gaps while preserving a readable pose. Small facial marks may also vanish, so concept studies can use fewer, larger relief marks. These are visual design cautions, not manufacturing or tolerance claims.
