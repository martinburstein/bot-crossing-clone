# Bot Crossing character corpus

This corpus records reference cues for an original Sowers helper prototype. It is not a Bot Crossing recreation, a style classifier, or a manufacturing specification. Structured trait evidence and source records are in [bot-crossing.json](./bot-crossing.json).

## Evidence boundary

- **User still, goat3:** Two small white astronaut-like helpers with round helmet/head masses, dark face screens, simple smiling eye marks, short crown antennae, compact torsos and stubby limbs. This still is user-supplied and its exact origin/version is not verified.
- **User preference anchor, goat4:** Keep the cream/orange/cocoa palette and cheerful helper personality as a separate Sowers preference. It is not evidence of Bot Crossing's palette, and no measured proportions are inferred.
- **Official product corroboration:** [Bot Crossing's official product page](https://emra.ai/product/bot-crossing) describes bots in a 3D project world, and says a bot waiting on a decision raises a question mark and waves. This corroborates context and behavior, not fine mesh geometry.
- **Local code corroboration:** `src/agents/astronauts.js`, `src/agents/faces.js`, and `src/agents/crew.js` document this clone's procedural helmet/visor/face, pack, antenna, lamp, status expressions, movement, work and wave motions. They are not a complete account of the public or commercial product.

## Actionable character direction

1. **Silhouette:** Oversized rounded head over a compact body; keep the shape recognizable at thumbnail size.
2. **Helmet:** Smooth protective hood with few broad seams; avoid copying the reference outline.
3. **Face:** A dark inset face window can carry a few bold, friendly marks. Original panel shape and expression language are needed.
4. **Torso:** Rounded tunic with a single large seed, leaf or pocket focal point.
5. **Limbs:** Short, rounded limb forms ending in mitten hands and broad boots; articulation is a proposed implementation, not established by the still. Keep gaps open and forms thick.
6. **Palette:** Consider goat4's cream body, orange accents and cocoa face as the user's preferred palette. Goat3 itself is predominantly pale with a dark screen. Do not assert exact ratios.
7. **Personality:** Patient, curious and gently upbeat. This is an interpretation from the supplied smiling still, not a product promise.
8. **Motion:** Prototype walking, tending/carrying, a work pose, and a clear stop-and-wave attention gesture. Official corroboration applies to the decision/wave behavior; local code supports that clone's broader motion vocabulary.
9. **Expression range:** Content, curious, focused, waiting, surprised and tired are useful states, whether communicated by screen marks or pose.
10. **Detail budget:** Use broad marks and sparse accents; fine bolts and tiny marks will disappear at small scale and are weak candy features.

## Blend guidance

- **High Bot influence:** Retain the compact friendly helper silhouette, protective rounded head, expressive face window and broad work/attention gestures. Translate every shape into an original Sowers design.
- **Medium Bot influence:** Use the soft helper proportions and a simple display, but shift the head contour, accent language and gestures toward garden tools, seed forms and the cream/orange/cocoa preference anchor.
- **Low Bot influence:** Keep only the approachable helper role and readability. Use a leaf hood, fixed sculpted face or non-screen expression, and omit thin crown antennae and suit-like detailing.

## Candy and originality guardrails

Thin antennae, tiny tips, narrow gaps, small knobs and fine raised marks can be fragile, vanish at product scale or complicate demolding. Favor connected rounded masses and broad shallow marks. Any candy render is a concept study, not evidence of tolerances or manufacturability. Do not copy Bot Crossing meshes, exact facial artwork, insignia or source assets.

## Unknowns

The still's source and version are not independently verified. The official page's text does not establish exact bot geometry, materials or proportions. Local implementation details only describe this clone. This corpus has no measured dimensions, color ratios, physical testing or candy tolerance data.

Campfire resolution: Goat4 cream/orange/cocoa stays invariant at every influence level. Readability across viewing scales is a Sower design test inferred from product context, not an official source requirement.
