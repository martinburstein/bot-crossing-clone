# Mars Mission character corpus

Reference-only visual research for original Sowers character direction. This is not a copied minifigure specification or an empirical style score. The supplied masked Mars Mission figure is the primary visual anchor; Goat 4 is a separate user preference anchor for cream/orange/cocoa warmth and cute helper energy, not a measured mixture.

## Sources and confidence

- **U1 — supplied `goat.png`**: direct observation of the user-provided front-view figure. Image has an ivory/white suit, broad cream helmet shell, raised amber-gold visor, charcoal-edged gray face covering, yellow eye opening with sharply angled dark brows/eyes, white arms, orange chest harness with dark linework and a round orange central device, small dark equipment details, and simple white legs. Front view only; no reliable side/back construction or articulation evidence.
- **U2 — supplied `goat4.png`**: direct observation of a separate Sowers concept preference image. Cream shell and body, orange crown stripe and side panels, cocoa face window, pale curved eyes, short rounded body/limbs, gentle friendly expression. It is a palette/personality anchor, not Mars source evidence or measured blend.
- **L1 — official LEGO 7693 instructions**, https://www.lego.com/cdn/product-assets/product.bi.core.pdf/4523183.pdf; local page-3 rendering `docs/sowers/references/lego-7693-official-page3.png`. Primary corroboration: page 3 depicts a white helmet, amber hinged visor, yellow head with black eye graphic, white torso with orange markings. This supports the broad helmet/color grammar, not every detail of U1 or exact balaclava variant.
- **R1 — United Brick Co mm002 listing**, https://atlbrick.com/products/mars-mission-astronaut-with-helmet-and-balaclava-mm002-1. Retail reference corroboration visually checked: raised amber visor; gray printed face covering with dark border, yellow eye opening and orange harness on white suit. Secondary source, not official LEGO evidence.
- **H1 — historical research note**: `docs/sowers/research.md` records that the exact balaclava catalog page could not be independently verified during earlier research; distinguish that uncertainty from the currently inspected retail photograph and official 7693 instructions.

## Blend guidance and candy-study pitfalls

- **High Mars influence:** broad cream/white helmet and suit, raised amber visor accent, clearly covered lower face with visible eyes, bold orange chest tool/harness. Keep the exact facial mood flexible.
- **Medium Mars influence:** retain a pale enclosing head and warm visor cue; reduce printed-looking detail, soften the mask contrast, and simplify the harness to a single focal tool.
- **Low Mars influence:** keep only a small warm helmet/mission cue or one explorer prop. Do not let a low blend accidentally retain the entire Mars silhouette and mask system.
- Candy simplification should preserve the outer helmet rim, separate visor brow, eye aperture, mask boundary, orange torso focal point, and rounded limb silhouette. Avoid fusing visor into one orange blob, turning the face into an unreadable dark slab, erasing mask/eye contrast, or using sugar texture that hides the simple gear shapes. The all-orange candy render in the preference study is a material study only; it is not evidence for Mars palette proportions.

No dimensions, measured ratios, or color percentages are inferred from these images. LEGO imagery and the retail photograph remain research references only; do not reuse as runtime textures, copied artwork, logos, or geometry.

Campfire resolution: source gray/yellow mask and circular equipment remain observations, not mandatory crossover ingredients. Default to the cocoa face and original seed mark. Mars influence can reshape brow, lower mask contour and suit structure without layering every reference detail.
