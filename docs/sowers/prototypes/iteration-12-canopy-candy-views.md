## Output review

The sheet presents one orange candy in front, side, and back views on a warm cream ground. The front carries a broad shallow recessed face window with two arch reliefs, one seed relief, connected mitten tabs, and broad boots with a shallow notch. The profile is substantially slimmer than the front and reads as a gently rounded slab. The back is plain, with no face or seed; its broad canopy and boot outline are similar to the front. All three views have nearly equal height.

The views are not fully consistent: the profile is not a strict orthographic projection, the near mitten is prominent and occludes part of the torso edge, and its canopy/boot contours differ subtly from the front and back. Fine surface grain and soft highlights remain visible despite the matte direction. These are illustrated concept views, not verified orthographic or manufacturing geometry. They do not establish dimensions, tooling, tolerances, demolding, or physical relief readability.
