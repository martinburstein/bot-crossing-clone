# Design Iteration 09 — reviewed corpus

All three fresh Luna Medium corpus tasks reached terminal status, then the same three challenged peers: Mars reviewed Bot, Bot reviewed Wobbly, Wobbly reviewed Mars. Coordinator parsed all JSON and checked every trait source reference resolves. Accepted corpus: Mars12traits/5sources, Wobbly11traits/5sources (including2distinctofficialscreens), Bot14traits/6sources. Reference observations are not animation or hidden-geometry proof.

Resolved changes: fixed cream/orange/cocoa anchor at every recipe; corrected Bot limb articulation to proposed implementation; labeled scale readability as design inference; retired low-influence palette drift. Source gray/yellow Mars face and circular gear do not automatically transfer into the new character. Default cocoa face and pale broad eyes remain; stronger Mars can introduce a protective brow and lower mask contour. A new original seed chest mark replaces planet/device artwork.

Composition rule: high influence establishes the leading form; medium contributes one or two cues; low contributes one restrained supporting cue. Recipe numbers are authored art-direction weights, not measured style attribution. For candy use three broad front relief groups: face/eyes, seed/chest, limb separation; helmet/brow belong to the outer shape group. No antenna dependency, disconnected mitten islands or fine strap prints.
