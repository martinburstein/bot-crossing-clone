The astronaut fleet

Eleven vehicles and side builds across eight 2007 Mars Mission sets. These are instruction images from LEGO's published manuals. Each card links to its exact source. Green alien figures or cargo occasionally present in an instruction step are excluded from the recreations.

The game uses original softened procedural geometry, with white hulls, amber canopies and orange wheels. Side-build names are descriptive. Source manifest and SHA-256 hashes
