THREE-NODE SOFTWARE-IN-THE-LOOP TEST

A swarm must survive losing a member.

This deterministic controller model is separate from the orbital power budget. Three simulated 10 W bench panels track a moving light source. Commands expire; local protection stays in charge. No physical hardware is connected.

THE STARTUP THESIS

Useful on Earth.
Ambitious in space.

Our first product is a measurable, repairable solar swarm testbed for research and education. The long-term technology is autonomous modular collection in space. Affordable electricity for people is the goal; terrestrial solar and storage are the benchmark to beat.

Read the investment case ↗
01

Prove one • bench

Two nominal 5 W panels, manual installation, local pointing and metered control power. Source-priced parts and a revised dual-rail supply. Physical assembly and calibration remain pending.

02

Prove many • coordination

Commission one node before building three. Measure net energy against a fixed reference, disconnect one node, and record fault recovery. Expand only when cost and measurement evidence support it.

03

Earn orbit • gated R&D

Only after deployment, thermal, radiation, communications and lifecycle economics pass independent review. No launch date or flight readiness claim.
